Featured Case Study: E-Commerce Platform Redesign

The Challenge

A client's outdated e-commerce platform was experiencing high cart abandonment rates and poor mobile conversion. They needed a complete redesign with improved user experience and modern technology stack.

My Role

As the lead developer, I was responsible for architecture planning, frontend development, and integration with payment gateways. I worked closely with a UX designer and backend developer to deliver the complete solution.

The Solution

I implemented a responsive React frontend with a Node.js backend, optimized for performance across all devices. Key improvements included a streamlined checkout process, real-time inventory updates, and enhanced product search functionality.

Results

The redesigned platform achieved a 35% reduction in cart abandonment, 42% increase in mobile conversions, and 28% improvement in overall site performance metrics.
